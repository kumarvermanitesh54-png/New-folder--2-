Nitesh's Tic Tac Toe Game - How to Play

Starting the game (first Turn)

  • The game always begins with player 'X' taking the first turn.
  • After every click, the turn will automatically switch (from 'X' to 'O' and back to 'X').

Reset and restart features

  • If you wish to restart the game from the beginning at any point, simply click the "Reset Game" button.
  • Resetting will clear the entire board and restart the move count back to zero.

Winner Identification (Live Status)

  • As soon as a player wins the match, the winner's name ('X' or 'O') will be displayed in the heading area top of the screen.
  • In this game, the winner is awarded the titel of "KING👑," and the winning pattern's background colornwill turn green.
  • If all boxes are filled and no one has 3 row, the match is a draw.The heading will show "It's a Draw!🤝" and the background color will turn Red......

Real-time Score Tracking

  • The scores for Player X, Ties, and Player O are displayed at the button of the game. The score updates automatically every time a player wins.

Play Next Button

  • Use this button to start the next round. It clears the board while saving next current scores, allowing you to continue your winning streak.
